What We Offer

Comprehensive Skin Care

From routine skin checks to advanced treatments, we provide expert care for all your dermatological needs.

Medical Dermatology

Diagnosis and treatment of skin conditions including acne, eczema, psoriasis, and rashes.

Skin Cancer Screening

Comprehensive skin exams and early detection of melanoma and other skin cancers.

Mole Removal

Safe removal and biopsy of suspicious moles and skin growths.

Superficial Radiation Therapy

Non-surgical SRT treatment for non-melanoma skin cancers — a painless, scalpel-free option with excellent cure rates and minimal scarring.

Meet Your Physician

Your Skin, Our Expertise

Dr. Matthew Rowley, M.D.

Board-Certified Dermatologist

Dr. Matthew Rowley is a board-certified dermatologist with over 30 years of experience in medical and surgical dermatology. Born in Fort Worth and raised in Houston, Texas, Dr. Rowley began his academic career at the University of Texas at Austin, graduating Summa Cum Laude.

He earned his medical degree from Southwestern Medical School in Dallas in 1990 and completed his dermatology residency at the historic Charity Hospital in New Orleans, Louisiana.

After residency, Dr. Rowley moved to Lufkin, Texas, where he founded Dermatology of East Texas in 1994. For over three decades, he has provided compassionate, patient-centered care to the East Texas community and has become one of the region's most trusted dermatologists.
